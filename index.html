Saju basics

How Saju Palja is read

Saju Palja, also called the Four Pillars or Eight Characters, converts a birth moment into heavenly stems and earthly branches, then reads balance and movement through yin and yang, the five elements, ten gods, and luck pillars.

Four pillars, eight characters

The year, month, day, and hour each have one heavenly stem and one earthly branch. Together they form the eight characters used as the base of a Saju birth chart.

Stems, branches, and five elements

Each character carries wood, fire, earth, metal, or water, along with yin or yang. The reading looks at which energies are strong, missing, supported, or in tension.

Day Master and ten gods

The day stem is treated as the reference point. The ten gods describe how other stems relate to it, such as peers, expression, resources, pressure, structure, and support.

Luck pillars and time flow

Luck pillars show changing ten-year phases. They do not replace choice, but they can frame why certain themes feel louder during one season of life than another.

Balance over fixed fortune

A practical Saju reading is less about declaring good or bad luck and more about noticing balance: what is overused, what is underused, and what habits can restore rhythm.
